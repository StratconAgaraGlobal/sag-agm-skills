## What the ASI review says

- ASI Performance Standard V3.1 has 11 Principles and 62 criteria. Measurement matters most under Principle 4 (Material Stewardship), 5 (GHG), 6 (Emissions, Effluents and Waste), 7 (Water) and parts of 11.
- Criterion 5.2 (primary smelting): mine-to-metal intensity below 11 t CO2e / t Al, or at least a 10% reduction over the previous three reporting periods. Criterion 5.3: a 1.5 °C-aligned reduction pathway. So electricity, fuel, production and process data matter as much as stack gas.
- Table 1 maps E+H instruments to ASI areas ("covered" means the capability was shown, not that it satisfies a criterion). Table 2 lists 14 data items still to close: electricity source and use, production tonnes, PFC (CF4, C2F6) and anode-effect data, anode consumption, solid fuel mass and calorific value, water balance, bauxite residue (criterion 6.6), fluoride in water, fugitive pot-room HF, spent pot lining, dross (criterion 6.8), other wastes incl. coal ash / FABA, spills (criterion 6.3), and data integrity / audit trail.
- Site named for the survey: Bintan / Galang Batang. The client is not named in the document.
- Clarifications asked of E+H: current SPARING connectivity approval (the one shown was issued 7 Oct 2021 with three-year validity); the KLHK SPARING classification for the Galang Batang site (alumina refining and smelting are not listed categories); audit-trail functions of the Memograph RSG45; local service for gas analysers.
- Next steps proposed: confirm the client's ASI Certification Scope; joint Bintan / Galang Batang site survey; E+H response to Table 2; agree the data architecture; GHG measurement and calculation package; indicative bill of measurement points; specialist introductions.
- The document states it is a technical preparation document, not an ASI certification opinion or legal determination.

## SPARING (KLHK)

SPARING = Sistem Pemantauan Kualitas Air Limbah Secara Terus Menerus dan Dalam Jaringan (continuous, online wastewater-quality monitoring). Regulation P.93/MENLHK/SETJEN/KUM.1/8/2018 as amended by P.80/MENLHK/SETJEN/KUM.1/10/2019. Parameters: pH, TSS, debit (flow), COD, NH3-N. E+H's eligible-industry list: mining, rayon, pulp and paper, upstream petrochemicals, palm oil, industrial estates (Kawasan Industri), textiles, oleochemicals, oil and gas exploration, petroleum processing.
