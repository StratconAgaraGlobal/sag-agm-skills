01 - Logo

Two fixed builds

The logo is used unchanged: never recolour, redraw, stretch or add effects. Pick the build by the ground it sits on.

sag-logo.png Transparent interior: the diagonal stripe shows the ground. Use on white or PAPER only.
sag-logo-plated.png Flood-filled white behind the shield. Works on any ground, including PANEL.

Files: assets/logo/sag-logo.png, assets/logo/sag-logo-plated.png (471 x 560 px).

02 - Colour

Eleven roles, named by job

Colours are roles, not names. SAG Yellow is an accent only: the tagline and a handful of short rules.

Contrast: BODY on PAPER 8.2:1 - INK on PAPER 15.4:1 - PAPER on PANEL 12.6:1 - PALE on PANEL 8.5:1 - ACCENT on PANEL 8.9:1, but on PAPER only 1.7:1, so never set ACCENT text on a light ground.

03 - Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans, and nothing else

In Word and PowerPoint each weight is its own font family, so choose the weight by family name. Only 700 uses the bold flag; never set bold on ExtraBold. On the web, use the normal font-weight.

Horizontal only, always carrying "NAVIGATING COMPLEXITY, DELIVERING SIMPLICITY". Use these images, or regenerate them with scripts/cline.py. Never redraw it and never use it as bare decoration.

07 - Restraint

What keeps it looking like SAG

Do

Use yellow for the tagline and short rules, a few marks per page. Put PAPER or PALE text on PANEL. Keep every full-width element exactly on the 170 mm measure (302.667 mm on slides).

Don't

Set yellow text on a light ground (1.7:1), fill large areas with it, or add stripes, shadows, gradients, icons or clip art. Never redraw the logo or the Complexity Line.
