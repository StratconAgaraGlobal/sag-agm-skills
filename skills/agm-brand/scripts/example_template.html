01 - Emblem

One emblem, three flat builds

Eight-point star, crescent, ship on waves. It has no wordmark of its own: set "PT AGARA GLOBAL MARITIM" in type beside it. Never recolour beyond these three builds, and never redraw, stretch or add effects.

02 - Colour

Eleven roles, named by job

The same eleven roles as SAG, named by job. Two editions: Seafood (default) for sea cucumber, processing, export, trade and investors; Marine for FRP shipbuilding, Harvester vessels, charter and marine services. Brass is never text on a light ground.

ACCENT_ON_DARK is the accent as it appears on PANEL (tagline line 2, the rule and its dot, tile values). In Marine, labels and numbers on light use META, not the brass accent.

03 - Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans, and nothing else

In Word and PowerPoint each weight is its own font family, so choose the weight by family name. Only 700 uses the bold flag; never set bold on ExtraBold. On the web, use the normal font-weight.

04 - The AGM device

Photography under a flat veil

AGM has no Complexity Line. Its banner is photography under a flat PANEL veil (78 %), carrying a two-line tagline and a rule that ends in an accent dot. Generate it with scripts/graphics.py; never hand-draw it, and no gradients. Seafood uses the sea cucumber warehouse photo; Marine uses the Harvester vessel.

graphics.masthead(brand, out, date) - document masthead, 170 x 56 mm (the date is baked in, so regenerate per document)

graphics.closing(brand, out) - document closing band, 170 x 30.9 mm

graphics.deck_band(brand, out) - full-bleed deck band (square corners)

Every component, in order

Masthead, eyebrow / title / subtitle, three-bar rule, intro, tiles, callout, contents strip, section bands, numbered items with response boxes, kv and data tables with status marks, roadmap, sign-off row, notes, contact panel, closing band. Measure 170 mm, corner radius 2.5 mm; same component sizes as SAG. Several pages are compressed into one column here.

07 - Restraint

What keeps it looking like AGM

Do

Use the accent for short rules and the device, a few marks per page. Put PAPER or PALE text on PANEL. Keep every full-width element exactly on the 170 mm measure (302.667 mm on slides).

Don't

Set brass as text on a light ground, fill large areas with the accent, or add stripes, shadows, gradients, icons, clip art or pill tags. Never redraw the emblem or the device.
