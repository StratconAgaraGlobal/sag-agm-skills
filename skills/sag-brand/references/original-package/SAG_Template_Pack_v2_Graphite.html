Foundations — Type & Colour

One typeface carries the entire firm. Commissioned originally for the provincial government of Jakarta’s public identity, Plus Jakarta Sans provides Stratcon Agara Global with an authentic Indonesian institutional anchor. Seven weights (200 to 800) and matching italics cover every need—from ministerial petitions to PowerPoint pitch decks and 100+ page corporate dossiers. Hierarchy is commanded by weight jumps, eliminating the need for a secondary font family.

Contrast worth remembering: graphite on yellow is 11.4:1 and white on slate is 13.8:1, so both carry text at any size. Yellow on white is 1.6:1 and gold on white 2.4:1 — neither may ever be used for type. Bronze is the warm value that does.

Send the printer the artboard at 96 × 61 mm with crop marks, CMYK, 300 dpi, fonts outlined. The yellow edge must bleed — if it stops at the trim line a 0.5 mm mis-cut shows white. If gold foil is used, it replaces the rule only; never foil the shield, because the mark already carries its own yellow.

Two contrast notes. Warm ivory on forest green is 9.6:1 and deep green on ivory is 10.4:1, so both directions carry text safely. But pale sage, soft terracotta and muted gold are rules only — each falls below 3:1 on forest green, so none of them may ever be set as type.

Print this one on uncoated stock. The earth palette dulls badly under gloss lamination, and the ivory is the point — a coated white would fight it. If a finish is wanted, use a soft-touch matt. The antique gold divider can be foiled, but the ESG rules must stay as printed colour, because their relationship to each other is what carries the meaning.
